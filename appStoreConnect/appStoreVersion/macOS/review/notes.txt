No sign-in is required, and the app works fully offline.

To try it: type or paste text in the left pane. It translates as you type (or press Command-Return). Pick languages from the two menus in the toolbar. Languages not yet downloaded show a download prompt from macOS.

New in 1.3: for the first 3 days after the first launch, every feature is unlocked, including Pro. After that, the free plan gives 5 translations a day, and Pro (a Yearly subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase) removes the limit and unlocks Best quality, pronunciation and read aloud, and Saved phrases. Locked features show a small PRO tag.

Because a new install is in its first 3 days, everything is unlocked during review. The Pro plans can be opened and purchased at any time from Translate Offline › Upgrade to Pro…, the card at the bottom of the sidebar, or Settings › Pro, which also has Restore Purchases.

Settings › General › Quality offers "Best quality" on Macs with Apple Intelligence. It uses the Apple Intelligence translation models and also runs on-device and offline.
