Translate Offline is a fast, privacy-focused translation app that works without an internet connection. 

• Offline translation — use it anywhere, even with no Wi-Fi
• Private by design — your text stays on your device
• Quick input — type or paste and get instant results
• Simple workflow — swap languages, copy results, and keep going. 

Supported languages include: English, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Korean, and more.

Great for travel, studying, and everyday writing.
