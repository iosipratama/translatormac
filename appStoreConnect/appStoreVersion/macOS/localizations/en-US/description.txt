Translate text on your Mac with no internet needed. Nothing you type leaves your Mac.

Type or paste, and the translation appears as you type. Use it for work emails and documents you'd rather not upload, for language study, or on a flight or train.

FEATURES
• Translates as you type, or press Command-Return
• Detects the language for you
• Recent translations, to find past ones fast
• No account and no sign-up
• Made for Mac: menu commands, keyboard shortcuts, and light and dark mode

TRANSLATE OFFLINE PRO
• Unlimited translations
• Best quality with Apple Intelligence, for more natural translations that still run offline (needs a Mac with Apple Intelligence)
• Pronunciation and read-aloud: see how to say words in languages like Japanese and Chinese, and hear them spoken
• Saved phrases, to keep the ones you reuse

LANGUAGES
Arabic, Chinese, Dutch, English, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, and Vietnamese. Download each one once, in the app or in System Settings, and it stays on your Mac.

FREE AND PRO
Everything is unlocked for your first 3 days. After that, the free plan gives you 5 translations a day, and Pro removes the limit. Pay once with Lifetime, or choose Yearly.

The Yearly plan renews automatically unless it's canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it anytime in your Apple Account settings.
