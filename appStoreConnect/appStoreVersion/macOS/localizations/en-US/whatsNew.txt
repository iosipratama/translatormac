Simpler language names

Languages now read as "Danish" and "Spanish" instead of "Danish (Latin, Denmark)", with Chinese (Simplified) and Chinese (Traditional) clearly separated. The list also keeps itself up to date as macOS adds new languages.

Also in this update

• Copy a translation with a single click, from the main window or from History
• Your language choices are remembered between launches
• History shows the language pair and when each translation happened
• Download a missing language directly from the app
• Clearer messages when a language pair isn't supported or isn't downloaded yet
• The panels no longer resize when a translation arrives
• Press Command-Y to open History
• Improved VoiceOver support throughout
